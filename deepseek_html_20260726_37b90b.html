Demand the resignation of the Road Transport & Highways Minister and the Petroleum Minister for pushing mandatory ethanol blending that damages engines, raises costs, and leaves no choice.

⛽ Why We're Protesting

The government's aggressive ethanol blending mandate forces every petrol vehicle owner to use fuel mixed with up to 20% ethanol. While biofuel has its place, removing the choice of pure petrol hurts millions. Two-wheelers, older cars, and small engines suffer corrosion, reduced mileage, and costly repairs. Farmers and rural citizens bear the brunt of higher effective fuel prices.

We are not against ethanol. We are against forced blending without an opt-out. The ministers have ignored public hardship. Fuel prices remain unbearable.

📢 Our Demands

  • Stop mandatory ethanol blending immediately. Give citizens the right to choose pure petrol.
  • Restore E0 (pure petrol) at all fuel stations alongside blended options.
  • Bring fuel prices down to a reasonable level by reducing excessive taxes and blending costs.
  • Resignation of the Road Transport & Highways Minister for neglecting vehicle owners' plight.
  • Resignation of the Petroleum Minister for forcing a policy that harms common people.
